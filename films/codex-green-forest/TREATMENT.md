# TREATMENT — Chú bé Codex và bí mật khu rừng xanh

**Original film.** 30 seconds, Vietnamese title, 1280×720, 24fps. Watercolor Brush style from Lemo-Opuscar. Character-led story without narration; original procedural forest score.

## Three possible structures
1. **Wonder → invitation → revelation (selected):** Codex sees a golden butterfly, follows it, discovers the forest's glowing path.
2. **Lost → listening → homecoming:** A lost boy follows a song to return home.
3. **Challenge → discovery → gift:** Codex overcomes a collapsed bridge, helping the forest awaken.

Selection: #1, because the butterfly creates an emotional throughline, supports watercolor's slow lateral camera grammar, and avoids synthetic spoken dialogue.

## Character continuity
Codex is a small eight-year-old explorer with tousled dark-brown hair, rust-orange explorer jacket, navy backpack, indigo trousers, a tiny amber scarf, child-sized proportions and an inquisitive expression. Amber butterfly has two broad wings and two smaller lower wings. No franchise connection or borrowed artwork.

## Timeline and camera
- **00–04.5** Forest journal painting reveals sky, translucent moss, an opening title. Codex pauses at the edge.
- **04.5–11** Golden butterfly appears, he follows cautiously. First smooth dolly.
- **11–18** Long lateral tracking shot through layered tree washes, ferns, leaves and mist. Character walks in cycle, feet planted.
- **18–24** Camera moves nearer to elder tree; butterfly waits by trunk hollow. Codex kneels to see the warm glow.
- **24–30** Hundreds of fireflies bloom around the elder tree. Slow pull-back reframes the woodland. End with a short hopeful line.

## Medium rules
Warm fibrous paper, translucent pigment, leaf and bark made of individually seeded brush ribbons, mild variable-width dry-brush texture, edge pooling, atmospheric perspective and sparse handwritten journal typography. No generic vector cartoon look, hard UI panels, title-card dissolves or rapid cuts.

## Sound design
Warm acoustic/plucked notes, gentle wind and birdsong, soft footsteps, magical bell cues at 5.1, 20.5, 24.6 seconds. All synthesized, no remote audio/licensing dependencies.

## QC
Must be actually rendered using upstream core/render/video.mjs + Playwright on GitHub Actions. Verify ffprobe reports a 30s 1280×720 H264 MP4 with audio. Inspect five styleframe images. Any failures are reported, not relabeled as success.
