# TREATMENT — Chú bé Codex và bí mật khu rừng xanh

## Format
Original 30-second animated short, 16:9, 1280×720, 24 fps. Candidate upstream style: `watercolor`, subject to its visual grammar.

## Logline
An adventurous young boy follows a glowing golden butterfly into a green forest. Each step awakens an ancient secret hidden among the roots.

## Character bible
- Codex: eight-year-old explorer, tousled dark hair, rust-orange jacket, indigo shorts, navy backpack, expressive eyes, natural child proportions; identity and costume consistent between scenes.
- Butterfly: small amber luminous wings, a gentle halo, and recognizable two-lobed silhouette.

## Shot-by-shot beats
- **00–05**: A cotton-paper landscape paints itself: layered trees, morning mist, sunbeams. Codex arrives at the forest edge and pauses.
- **05–11**: A golden butterfly emerges. Codex notices it, turns and smiles, then takes a tentative step.
- **11–18**: Side-tracking shot through layered foliage. Ferns and moss paint in as Codex follows. Walking must have believable planted feet, not cutout sliding.
- **18–24**: The butterfly hovers above an old tree root. Codex kneels, reaches out, and discovers a glowing line tracing a secret path.
- **24–30**: Pull back to reveal hundreds of fireflies forming a luminous trail deep into the forest. Codex looks on in wonder. End on a visual hook.

## Camera and motion
Establishing wide, medium tracking, intimate detail, final wide. Respect the upstream style's camera vocabulary: lateral dolly, slow push-in, mist wipe, pullback. No arbitrary title-card cuts.

## Sound
Soft wind, birds, rustling leaves, footsteps, gentle butterfly chimes, understated acoustic score. Narration optional.

## Acceptance criteria
- Rendered with **upstream Lemo-Opuscar**, with command/log evidence.
- Consistent character design and proportions across all shots.
- No foot sliding, blank frames, broken typography or abrupt composition errors.
- MP4 with valid H.264 video and audio; target 30 s at 1280×720, 24 fps.
- At least five frames inspected; deliver sources, render log and QC notes.
